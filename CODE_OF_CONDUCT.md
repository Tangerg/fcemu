# Code of Conduct

FC Emu is committed to a focused, respectful and technically rigorous community.

## Expected behavior

- Be clear, patient and constructive.
- Critique claims and code, not people.
- Distinguish measured hardware evidence from memory, preference and speculation.
- Credit sources, test authors and prior work.
- Respect legal boundaries around commercial ROMs and proprietary material.
- Help make discussions accessible to contributors with different backgrounds and experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussion or repeatedly ignoring maintainer direction.
- Sharing, requesting or facilitating unauthorized commercial ROM distribution.
- Exploiting a private vulnerability before coordinated disclosure.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues and other spaces where someone
represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions or temporarily/permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Tangerg). Security vulnerabilities follow
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Reports will be considered in context, with privacy preserved as far
as reasonably possible.
